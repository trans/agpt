Precondition encoder

The precondition strand gave AGPT context from before a node's prefix. A GRU encoder ran over d_pre=16 characters preceding the prefix, sampled as one corpus instance per node per fire from a sidecar file, and its state was residual-injected through W_pre at layer 0 (before or after LN1). Tests used the v1 trainer via bin/agpt_experiment. At d=64/L=2 (25 ep, 3 seeds, 12 runs) the GRU tied the baseline (byte PPL 22.687) and mean-pooling was worse (22.941). With the d=128/L=6/pd=1/128 ep recipe the GRU regressed slightly (6.474 vs 6.366), with tied train loss and 7.2x the wall time. Closed 2026-06-02 as null. The design adds a term from outside AGPT's aggregation graph, so it would not extend AGPT even if it had helped.

Code: branch precondition, tag exp/precondition. Key files are notes/seq-len-extension/precondition.md, src/cuda/agpt_precondition_kernels.cuh, src/cuda/agpt_precondition_sidecar.cuh, src/tools/agpt_build_precondition_sidecar.cr and src/cuda/agpt_train.cu (YAML experimental.precondition.d_pre). Run dirs are rnd/precondition-step1/ and rnd/precondition-d128L6/ on the branch.
