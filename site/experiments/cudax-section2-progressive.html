CUDAX Section 2 progressive reruns

With the Section 2 event-count weighting restored in the CUDAX (v2) trainer, what held-out PPL do progressive-growth schedules (divisions × epochs) and static full-prefix training reach on Shakespeare?

Opened 2026-05-26Updated 2026-05-27
THE ANSWER SO FAR

Re-baselined on the tail-heldout split. d64 L2 runs range from 8.25 rolling byte PPL (16x1to6) to 7.05 (16x25). At 6 epochs per stage, 64, 128 and 256 divisions (7.50, 7.35, 7.39) beat 16 divisions (7.98). The best run is d128 L6 static at 200 epochs: 6.16 rolling byte PPL (5.65 fixed-window).
